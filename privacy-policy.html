Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Us ("we," "our," or "us") builds the Us app — a private space for couples to answer daily questions, track milestones, and stay connected. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to run the app and improve it over time:

CategoryWhat it includes
Account & pairing infoA device or account identifier used to link you and your partner's private space, plus any invite code you create or redeem.
Content you addPhotos, captions, timeline entries, question answers, goals, and date ideas you and your partner create inside the app.
Purchase informationSubscription status and transaction details, handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your card number.
Push notification tokenA device token used to deliver reminders like daily questions, hugs, or milestone alerts.
Usage & analytics dataApp interactions (e.g. screens viewed, features used, crash reports) collected through analytics tools to help us understand what's working and fix bugs.

How we use your information

  • To create and sync your private, shared space with your partner.
  • To deliver the core features of the app: daily questions, timelines, goals, hugs, and milestones.
  • To send push notifications you've opted into.
  • To process and manage your subscription through Apple or Google's billing systems.
  • To understand usage patterns and improve app performance and features through aggregated analytics.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate important updates about the app.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers who help us run the app:

  • Apple App Store / Google Play — to process subscription payments and manage billing.
  • Analytics providers (e.g. Firebase Analytics or similar) — to understand app usage in aggregate. This data is generally anonymized or pseudonymized.
  • Push notification services (e.g. Firebase Cloud Messaging, Apple Push Notification service) — to deliver the notifications you enable.
  • Cloud storage & hosting providers — to securely store and sync your photos, answers, and timeline content between you and your partner.

Your conversations, photos, and answers are shared only with the partner you're paired with. We don't make your content public, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Data storage & security

Your content is stored on secure cloud infrastructure with encryption in transit. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices & rights

  • Access & export — you can view all the content you've added directly within the app.
  • Deletion — you can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.
  • Notifications — you can turn off push notifications at any time from your device settings.
  • Subscription management — subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings, where you can also cancel or view billing history.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws like the GDPR (EU/UK) or CCPA (California), including the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Children's privacy

Us is intended for adults in a relationship and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Data retention

We retain your content for as long as your account is active, so your timeline and history stay intact. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain it for legal or accounting purposes.

International users

Your information may be processed and stored on servers located outside your country of residence. By using the app, you consent to this transfer, in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise your data rights, reach out to us at itsjustus.app@gmail.com.
